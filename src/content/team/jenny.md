Originally from California, Jenny has called the Gettysburg area home since 2022. She is a nationally award-winning photojournalist, journalist, and professional photographer with more than 20 years of experience documenting people, places, and events. Her background has sharpened her skills in observation, research, visual documentation, and storytelling. She brings those skills to paranormal investigations, combining a critical eye with curiosity and a genuine interest in uncovering the history and stories behind unexplained phenomena.
