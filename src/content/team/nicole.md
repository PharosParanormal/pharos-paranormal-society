Originally from Baltimore, Nicole has called the Gettysburg area home for the past 13 years. She is a Licensed Professional Counselor and National Certified Counselor with a background in mental health, human behavior, and evidence-based practice. Her clinical work has strengthened her skills in observation, interviewing, research, and understanding the ways people perceive and interpret their experiences. She brings those skills to paranormal investigations with a thoughtful and grounded approach, balancing curiosity with critical evaluation. As the group's psychic medium, Nicole also uses intuitive and mediumistic impressions in the field to identify potential areas of activity, explore possible connections, and help guide investigations while keeping those impressions distinct from independently gathered evidence.

Outside of Pharos, Nicole is the creator of <a href="https://www.theparathist.net" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Parathist</a>, her own paranormal project.
